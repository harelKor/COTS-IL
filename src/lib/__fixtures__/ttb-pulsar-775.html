The Pulsar 775, produced by Thrifty Bot as its pioneering motor, delivers a powerful, compact standalone brushless DC motor engineered for compatibility with the Thrifty Nova. This innovative motor stands out with its compact build and adaptable mounting solutions, serving as a seamless substitute for the Kraken X44, perfect for FRC robots, industrial setups, and beyond.

As a brushless motor, it requires a compatible brushless controller to avoid potential damage from improper use.

Multiple mechanical features boost its mounting versatility and practicality, including:

  • A 1.375" bolt circle featuring 4 blind 10-32 tapped holes for straightforward installation.

  • SplineXS Shaft with a 10-32 threaded hole 1/2" deep for reliable accessory attachment.
  • Additional 775-pattern mounting with 4 blind M4 × 0.7 tapped holes on a 29mm bolt circle.

  • A 3mm deep 19mm mounting boss to ensure accurate alignment.

Key Features

  • Direct swap for Kraken X44

  • SplineXS Shaft for hassle-free assembly

  • Dual mounting configurations for flexibility

  • 14AWG silicon-coated phase wires

  • 24AWG Hall sensor wires with FRC JST connector, with potting on the crimps

Advantages for FRC

  • Superior Power: Optimized for non-propulsion mechanisms with high efficiency.

  • Easy Integration: Diverse mounting options streamline robot construction.

Specifications

  • Free Speed: 7500 RPM

  • Free Current: 2.7 A

  • Max Efficiency: 85%

  • Peak Power: 604 W

  • Efficiency at 40A: 80%

  • kT: 16.2 mNm/A

  • Stall Current: 189 A

  • Stall Torque: 3.1 Nm

  • Shaft Type: SplineXS (15T ISO, fits 8mm bearing)

  • Diameter: 42mm

  • Length: 2.85 inches (72.5mm)

  • Weight: 0.65 lbs (with wires)

  • Compatible Mounting Hole Patterns: 4x 10-32 on a 1.375" Bolt Circle, 4x M4 on a 29mm Bolt Circle

  • Pilot Boss Dimensions: 19mm

  • Motor Phase Wires: 18" long 14AWG silicon-jacketed

  • Sensor Cable: 18" long 24AWG with JST PH6 connector

  • 42 ticks/rev encoder via hall sensor feedback
