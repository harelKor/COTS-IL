Offering an assortment of gear types, chosen to provide the greatest versatility of gearing options to users, scomponent sizes were specifically chosen to provide a wide spread of ratio options useful for FRC designers. Larger size gears are available so designers can accomplish their entire reduction in a single stage where otherwise they would have used more than one.

All WCP gears are 20DP with a 14.5 degree pressure angle, and have the same overall width. This means it is easy to interchange gears in your design to change a ratio without modifying the overall system; true versatility.

SplineXL bore with MotionX pattern for use on SplineXL tubes and MotionX Accessories.
Made from 4140 Steel.
Overall width 0.500".
